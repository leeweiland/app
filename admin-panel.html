Daily Coach Check-in
Once a day per student (online/gym clients only), an AI reads back a few of their most recent chat messages and posts a short, friendly check-in into their group — encouragement or a quick question, grounded in what's actually been said. Generated using the prompt below.
The poller checks hourly, so a check-in lands within about an hour after the time above, not to-the-minute (a month is treated as 30 days).
Any message, video or photo in the student's group (from them or a coach) inside this window means a conversation is going, so no check-in that day. It goes out at the usual send time once things have been quiet this long. 0 turns this off.
If the rule says not to send (for example the student's question hasn't been answered yet), that student is skipped for the day and checked again next time.
Which staff member these check-in messages appear to come from. "Auto" picks whichever coach/admin happens to be in that student's group first — not predictable, and can vary group to group.
