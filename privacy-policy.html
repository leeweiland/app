Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

This policy explains what information the Pacific Rim Athletics app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. It applies to the app and the account you create to use it.

Information You Provide

  • Account info: name, email, phone number, password, and an optional profile photo.
  • Messages and media: chat messages, photos, and videos you send to your coach or training group, including in-app camera captures.
  • Body and training data: height, weight, age, and photos you submit for body-composition scans and food logging. Photos submitted for these features are analyzed by an AI vision service to estimate body-fat percentage or calorie/macro content.
  • Scheduling info: appointment times you book with your coach.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and usage info: your IP address, and an approximate location (city/region/country) derived from it at signup.
  • Push notification token: a device identifier used to deliver notifications about new messages, if you enable them.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate the app's core features: connecting you with your coach, delivering messages and media, generating your body-composition and nutrition estimates, scheduling sessions, and sending notifications. We do not sell your information, and we do not use your messages or photos to train AI models.

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate the app. Each only receives the information needed to perform its function:

  • Google Drive / Google Calendar — stores photos, videos, and call recordings you send, and manages scheduled sessions.
  • AI vision providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) — analyze body-scan and food-log photos to generate estimates, and assist coaches in drafting captions for content they choose to publish.
  • Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging — deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Daily.co — powers in-app video calls with your coach.
  • Railway — hosts the app and its data.

Coaches may, with your awareness, publish footage you've favorited to Pacific Rim Athletics' own social media accounts via Metricool; this is a separate, coach-initiated action and not automatic.

Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below; we'll remove it except where we're required to keep records for legal or safety reasons.

Your Choices

You can edit your profile information at any time in the app, disable push notifications in your device settings, and block or report other users directly from the chat (see our Terms of Use).

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. Media is stored in access-controlled cloud storage tied to your account and coaching relationships.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to lee@pacificrimathletics.com.
