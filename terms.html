Terms of Use & Community Guidelines

Last updated August 2026

These terms apply to your use of the Pacific Rim Athletics app, including the messaging features used to communicate with your coach and, where applicable, other members of your training group.

Zero Tolerance for Objectionable Content or Abusive Behavior

Pacific Rim Athletics has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior of any kind. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Harassment, threats, or abusive language directed at coaches or other users
  • Sexually explicit, hateful, or discriminatory content
  • Content that promotes violence, self-harm, or illegal activity
  • Spam, impersonation, or deliberately misleading content

By creating an account, you agree not to post, send, or share content that violates these guidelines, and you agree not to abuse, harass, or threaten any other user of the app.

Reporting & Blocking

Every message can be reported directly from the chat if you believe it violates these guidelines. You can also block another user at any time — once blocked, you will no longer see messages from that person, and they will not be able to message you.

Reports are reviewed by our team. Accounts found to violate these guidelines may be suspended or permanently removed at our discretion.

Contact

Questions about these terms, or anything you'd rather report directly, can be sent to lee@pacificrimathletics.com.
